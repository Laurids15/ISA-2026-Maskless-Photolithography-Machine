LITHOGRAPHY EXPOSURE — 1024 × 768

InFocus IN124 DMD, native resolution, strictly binary patterns generated in the page. Nominal 2.2 µm/px on the wafer at 5× zoom (calibrate with pattern 2).

This menu is projected onto the wafer too. Keep the UV LED off (or the green filter in) while the menu is open. Select a pattern to go to the black armed screen; from there you can change pattern, time and fill without coming back here.

SETTINGS

Fill (70–100 %) sets how much of the screen the patterns marked fill use, centred, with L-shaped marks at the corners of the area. Line widths, gaps and squares stay in exact pixels; only the layout grows (longer lines, larger patches, larger star, larger dose cells). Full-field patterns always use the whole screen.

The corner text is projected too. If the UV is on while the screen is armed, it exposes that corner of the wafer. At fill above about 85 % it lies inside the pattern area. Put it in the corner that is blocked by the housing, or switch it off (H).

GREYSCALE FLAT-FIELD CORRECTION (key X toggles)

Measured map, one row per line from the DMD top, values left to right (any unit, e.g. µW per tile from the probe). It is interpolated smoothly over the DMD. Every lit pixel brighter than the target is dimmed to the target using the measured grey curve (or gamma if that is off); pixels at or below the target stay at full. Gamma converts grey level to light: test it by running the probe with "Probe grey level" at e.g. 255, 200 and 150. Light ∝ (level/255)gamma. Then run the probe with the correction on: the tiles should come out flat. The pre-filled map is the mean of two 32 × 24 probes of 10 Nov without correction (10:58 and 13:05; tiles of 32 px, LED warmed up, low bandwidth, drift reference), neighbour-weighted smoothing (sigma 0.6 tiles), % of mean. Target 90. The camera map (greymap_camera_v4_64x48.csv) did not match the power meter.
