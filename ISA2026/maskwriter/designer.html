Help

Everything is drawn on the 1024 × 768 DMD pixel grid and exported 1:1 (white = lit mirror = exposed). Coordinates are in µm from the centre of the usable area, y pointing down, and are always rounded to whole pixels (lines of odd width sit on pixel centres so both edges fall on pixel borders).

Each object either adds light or cuts it away; objects lower in the list are drawn later. Use cut objects for holes and gaps.

Rectangle / circle: drag. Line / polygon: click the points, double-click or Enter to finish, Shift = 0/45/90°. Text: click, then type (5 × 7 pixel font, capitals and digits). Select: click, Shift-click to add, drag a box; drag to move; drag the yellow handles to resize. Arrows move by 1 px (Shift: 10 px). Wheel = zoom, middle button or Space-drag = pan.

Design grid (Shift+G): lines every N µm from the centre of the usable area (green; the centre lines are brighter, every 5th line medium). When zoomed out the grid is thinned in steps of 5 (status bar shows the spacing). Snap to grid puts new points, rectangle corners, circle centres, handle drags and moves on the grid; positions are still rounded to whole pixels, so a grid that is not a whole number of pixels gives up to ½ px deviation.

The dose ladder of 4 Nov: dark gaps narrower than ~3 px close, lit features grow ~2–3 px per edge next to big lit areas, isolated 2 px dots print at ~165 mJ/cm². The checks list flags features under 3 px.

“Send to exposure window” opens expose.html (same folder) the first time; drag that window to the projector and press F there. Later sends go to the same window. The design is auto-saved in this browser.
