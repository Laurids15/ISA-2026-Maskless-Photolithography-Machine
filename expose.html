MASK EXPOSURE — 1024 × 768

Load a mask (a PNG, ideally 1024 × 768 from the Mask Designer, or send it straight from the designer), set the exposure time, switch the greyscale correction on or off, arm, and press Space.

This menu is projected onto the wafer too. Keep the UV LED off (or the green filter in) while the menu is open. The armed screen is black except for a small status text in one corner.

Preview at 50 %: what the DMD will show (grey = dimmed by the correction). Dashed outline = usable area.

Pixels with brightness ≥ 128 are lit (mirror ON), the rest dark. Images of exactly 1024 × 768 are used as they are; smaller images are centred in the usable area. Nothing is scaled.

DOSE HELPER

Measure the power with the power meter on the sample while the screen shows white in the usable area only (key W on the armed screen), with the correction set as you will expose. Irradiance = power / area of the usable area (), i.e. the power density where you actually expose. Shift+W shows white on the whole DMD instead (then select "whole DMD" above). The dose ladder of 4 Nov gave nearly full thickness with 3 px gaps still open at ~155–170 mJ/cm².

Calibration data: usable area, correction map and grey curve

USABLE AREA

The right 128 px are left out because of the dark top-right corner (alignment v4).

GREYSCALE CORRECTION

Light map without correction, one row per line from the DMD top (any unit). It is interpolated over the DMD. Lit pixels brighter than the target are dimmed to the target with the grey curve; pixels at or below it stay full. Pre-filled: high-resolution map 10 Nov, mean of two 32 × 24 probes without correction (10:58 and 13:05), neighbour smoothing (sigma 0.6 tiles), target 90; grey curve: ramp 10 Nov 10:09 (32 levels, knee at grey 160). Re-measure both with the test viewer after any change of the optics.
