EXPOSURE CAMERA

Logitech C920 / C930e (any UVC camera). Use Chrome or Edge: they expose manual exposure, focus and white balance. Run this window on the laptop screen and the pattern viewer fullscreen on the projector.

CAMERA

Dynamic frame rate (exposure_dynamic_framerate) is not reachable from a browser, and it only acts in automatic exposure. In manual exposure, keep the exposure time at or below one camera frame (see the list), and pick a lower frame rate for longer exposures. The measured frame rate is shown top right.

EXPOSURE AND IMAGE (manual)

Exposure time is in units of 100 µs (333 = 33.3 ms). Use whole projector frames (16.7 / 33.3 / 50 ms) to avoid flicker and banding from the DMD.

µm per camera px
Example: measure between two crosses of pattern 02 that are 4 crosses apart (4 × 64 DMD px = 256 px ≈ 563 µm at 2.2 µm/px), or use a stage micrometer.

FOCUS METER

Turn the stand's focus knob slowly through focus. The score is image sharpness (gradient energy / brightness², on Max of RGB) in 128 × 128 px around each ROI marker (up to 4), or in the central 256 × 256 px without markers. Best focus = peak. More precise: note the knob position where the score falls to 80 % on each side, and set the middle. Markers at opposite edges of the field show tilt: their peaks come at different knob positions.

Fallback without Chrome controls 
LINUX (v4l2-ctl, run after the camera is opened):
  v4l2-ctl -d /dev/video0 --list-ctrls
  v4l2-ctl -d /dev/video0 -c auto_exposure=1 \
     -c exposure_time_absolute=333 -c exposure_dynamic_framerate=0 \
     -c gain=0 -c white_balance_automatic=0 -c white_balance_temperature=4000 \
     -c focus_automatic_continuous=0 -c focus_absolute=0 \
     -c sharpness=0 -c backlight_compensation=0 -c power_line_frequency=1
  Older kernels name them exposure_auto=1, exposure_absolute,
  exposure_auto_priority=0, white_balance_temperature_auto=0, focus_auto=0.

WINDOWS:
  OBS: add Video Capture Device > Properties > Configure Video >
  Camera Control: untick Auto for Exposure and Focus;
  Video Proc Amp: untick Auto for White Balance, set Gain.
  In Logitech software (Logi Tune), turn RightLight off.

MAC:
  Use an app that sets UVC controls (e.g. "Webcam Settings"),
  then open this page; Chrome on Mac exposes only some controls.
